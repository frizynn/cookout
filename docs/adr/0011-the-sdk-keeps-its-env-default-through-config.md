# 0011: The SDK keeps its env default through config

2026-11-22. At `876d300`, `createCookoutClient` defaulted its URL and token to `COOKOUT_URL` and `COOKOUT_TOKEN` (story 7 of mattpocock/cookout#1). The rewrite makes `config` the only reader of `process.env`, and the audit proposed dropping the default, which would change behaviour. Instead the default is kept through a plain-TS reader of those two variables in `config`'s `index.ts`, which returns nothing in a browser; the SDK uses it when no `url` or `token` is passed. `config` stays the one owner of the environment and the SDK's behaviour doesn't change. Source: mattpocock/cookout#17, decision 6 (chief of staff's spec agent, 2026-11-22), under wiki `chief-of-staff/cookout/decision-perfect-ts-rewrite.md` (2026-11-22).
