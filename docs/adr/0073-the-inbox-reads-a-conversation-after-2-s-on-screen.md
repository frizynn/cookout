# The Inbox reads a conversation after 2 s on screen, not on open

2026-11-24 (mattpocock/cookout#206, spec #201). Opening a thread or a channel has always acked its newest message at once, so a glance counts as reading. In the Inbox that clears a mention you clicked by mistake, or one you flicked past with the keyboard, before you have dealt with it. Matt asked for Slack's rule (Inbox grilling on the board, 2026-11-23): "if you scroll to it, and it's visible on your screen as the main thing for a while ... then it marks it as read". We decided that the Inbox's selected conversation acks nothing when it opens. Once it has shown the same newest message for 2 s continuously, while the page is visible and its window has focus, it acks that message. Losing visibility or focus, or selecting another row, restarts the count. A message arriving live in the open conversation restarts it too, and the next ack covers it. Scrolling the list never reads anything.

Three things read a row at once instead: a reply of your own arriving in the conversation (in a thread the server already moves your read position when you post; the conversation acks its newest message as well, which also covers a channel conversation), the header's Mark read button, and nothing else. After Mark read or a reply, a "Marked as read" note with Undo shows for 5 s. Undo marks the conversation unread (ADR `docs/adr/0053-marking-a-message-unread-moves-a-read-position-back.md`) from its first message that was unread when the row was selected, which puts the row back. The 2 s rule shows no note, since Mark unread puts a row back just as well. The header's Mark unread does the same as Undo, or marks unread from the newest message if nothing was unread when the row was selected. While the row stays selected after either, the 2 s rule doesn't run again, so Mark unread sticks; selecting the row again later starts afresh.

## Consequences

- **The thread panel and the channel view are unchanged.** Opening a thread from a channel, or a channel, still acks at once, and they still ack each live message while open. Only the Inbox counts time on screen. The `board` store's thread store has a second way to subscribe, `subscribeWithoutAcking`, which loads, follows and reloads the thread but acks nothing; the Inbox's reader calls the store's `ack` when the rule says so.
- **Time and focus are seams.** The rule waits on a `Clock` (`browserClock` in the browser, on the page's timers) and reads the `page-focus` store (visibility and focus through an adapter). Route tests bring a clock they move on by hand and a focus they blur and hide by hand, so the 2 s and 5 s pass without waiting and without jsdom's focus, which never changes.
- **Selecting a row only shows it.** Nothing is read until 2 s have passed, so moving through the rows with the keyboard (#207) reads only the one you stop on.
- **The rule lives in the web.** The server, the SDK and the CLI are unchanged: `cookout inbox` and every other client read and ack as before.

## Considered options

- **Ack on open, as a thread does.** Rejected: it is what Matt asked to change.
- **Ack once the conversation is scrolled into view (an intersection observer).** Rejected for now: the conversation sits in its own pane beside the list, so being selected and on a focused page is what "on screen as the main thing" means here. A conversation taller than its pane still acks its newest message after 2 s, which matches Slack.
- **Count the 2 s only once, from the first open.** Rejected: a background tab, or another window in front, would then read a conversation nobody looked at.
