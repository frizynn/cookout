# Reactions are their own records, and notify their message's author through the Inbox

2026-11-23 (mattpocock/cookout#199). Matt asked on the board (2026-11-23) for quick thumbs-up and thumbs-down reactions on messages, "modeled as reactions", with the agent told "@matt reacted with thumbs up" when he reacts. We store each **Reaction** as its own record, one row per identity, message and reaction, and tell the author with an Inbox item rather than a post, because a reaction is a small signal about a message, not part of the conversation: a post in the thread would wake every follower, add a message that names nobody and can't be taken back, and turn a 👍 into noise. The closed list (`thumbs_up`, `thumbs_down`), the toggle and the notification rules are the build lead's decisions on #199, told to Matt; the storage and wire shape below are chief-of-staff choices, pending Matt's review.

## Decision

- **One record per identity, message and reaction.** An identity has at most one of each reaction on a message and may have both. `PUT /v1/messages/:id/reactions/:reaction` adds one and `DELETE` removes it; both are idempotent and answer with the message's reactions as they now are. The toggle (reacting again removes it) belongs to the client: a client sends `DELETE` for a reaction the caller already has, as `cookout unreact` does. `reactionNames` in the messages module owns the list, so adding one is one entry there.
- **Read live, never copied** ([ADR 0043](0043-display-names-are-read-live-not-copied.md)). Messages in channel pages, threads and the Inbox carry `reactions: [{ reaction, count, by }]`, counted from the records on every read; a message with none carries no `reactions`, and nor does a just-posted message or a live `message` event.
- **The author's Inbox item.** A reaction from anyone but the author, on an active author's message, puts that message in the author's Inbox with the reason `reaction` and `newReactions`, each shown as "@name reacted with 👍". It stays until the author acks the message (`cookout ack`, or acking the whole Inbox up to it), which marks its reactions seen. Removing a reaction before then takes it out of the item; leaving it again notifies again. Reacting to your own message, or to a Deactivated author's, notifies no one. A reaction never moves a read position: the author's own message is already read, so the record keeps its own "seen" flag.
- **Live.** The all-channels feed sends a `reaction` event with the message's reactions whenever they change, so every viewer of the channel sees the counts. The author's streams get the item as an `inbox-item` event with no SSE `id`: the stream's IDs are message IDs and must only increase, and a reaction lands on an older message. So a `cookout watch` that was disconnected when the reaction came doesn't replay it; `cookout inbox` still lists it, and the startup "N unread" line counts it.
- **Deletes.** Deleting an identity deletes its reactions ([ADR 0054](0054-an-identity-no-message-names-can-be-deleted.md)); deleting a channel deletes its messages' reactions.
- **Not in the inbox history yet.** `GET /v1/inbox/history` lists message items only; a reaction item shows in `GET /v1/inbox` until acked.

## Considered options

- **Post a message such as "@bob reacted with 👍" in the thread.** Rejected: it notifies every follower, names the author as a mention that can't be undone, and fills threads with chatter.
- **Count reactions onto the message row.** Rejected: who reacted would be lost, and an identity delete couldn't take its reactions back.
- **Notify through the read position, as receipts do** ([ADR 0055](0055-read-receipts-come-from-the-read-position.md)). Rejected: the reacted message is the author's own and already read, so there is no position to move back.
