Web UI: an open thread's header has an actions menu, opened by right-clicking the header, by its More actions (⋯) button, or by the context-menu key or Shift+F10 on the focused heading. It offers Close or Reopen, whichever applies, as the thread's own button does; Mark unread, which marks the thread's top-level message unread and puts it back in the Inbox; and Copy link, which copies the thread's full URL.
