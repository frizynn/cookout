In the web message, reply and Inbox reply boxes, `@ ` followed by a space, as dictation types it, keeps the mention list open with the top match highlighted, and `@ g` filters it as `@g` does. Tab or Enter writes `@name ` in place of the `@ `. A plain "at" with no `@` still opens nothing.
