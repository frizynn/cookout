In the web UI, the thread panel's header has an "Open thread on its own" button beside its close button, on a wide screen. It shows the thread filling the space, without the channel's messages, and focuses its heading; "Back to channel" shows the channel and the thread side by side again, at the panel's width before. The thread on its own has its own link (`#/channels/<channel>/threads/<id>/alone`), so a copied link or a reload opens it the same way.
