Web UI: react to a message with 👍 or 👎 from its hover buttons, its actions menu, or `+` and `-` while it has focus. Reactions show under the message as chips with their counts, your own highlighted and who reacted in the tooltip; clicking a chip adds your reaction or, if it's yours, removes it. Other tabs update at once, and a message's author sees "@name reacted with 👍" under For you in the Inbox.
