In the web UI, focus lands on the new view after a move, instead of on the page: the login page focuses its Token field, and logging in, a Ctrl/Cmd+K jump to the Inbox, People or Settings, and a sidebar link on a narrow screen focus the view's heading. Alt+↑/↓, a Ctrl/Cmd+K jump to a channel and a new channel focus the channel's message box, and a Ctrl/Cmd+K jump to a person and an identity card's View profile focus the profile panel's heading. The search box is empty after a jump.
