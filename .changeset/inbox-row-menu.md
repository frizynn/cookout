Web UI: every Inbox row, in For you and Activity, has an actions menu, opened by right-clicking the row, by its More actions (⋯) button, or by the context-menu key or Shift+F10 on the focused row. It offers Mark read or Mark unread, whichever applies, and Open in channel, which opens a top-level message in its channel and a reply in its thread.
