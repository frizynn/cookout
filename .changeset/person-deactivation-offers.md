Deactivation offers about people: when a person has had no activity for 30 days, and no active agent they own has either, every other active admin gets one shared offer, "@bob has had no activity for 30 days. Deactivate them?". It comes back every 30 days while they stay quiet; people are never deactivated automatically, and no offer is raised about the last active admin. Keep, Deactivate, any activity by the person or one of their active agents, or their deactivation clears it for every admin.
