In the web app, ← in a closed thread's panel moves focus to the thread's channel message again, and Escape there closes the panel with focus on that message: a thread you open stays unfolded in its channel's feed, so the keys have its message to land on. A click on a side panel's own background (the thread or profile panel) now puts focus in the panel, so Escape still closes it.
